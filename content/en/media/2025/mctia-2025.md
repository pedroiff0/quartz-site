# 🛰️ MCTIA 2025

> [!note] Summary
> Açaí Institute’s Science and Technology Show in Belém (PA) — 1st place in Exact Sciences with an AI algorithm that recovers 99.7% of the information lost by satellite tracks, classifying the team for Young Science 2026.

## 🗓️ About the event

 Page dedicated to participation in the XVI Exhibition of Science and Technology of the Açaí Institute (MCTIA)**, in Belém (PA) — the largest scientific fair in the North.

 With [Maycon Jorge Deláqua da Silva](https://mayconjdelaqua.vercel.app/) and Arthur Miquelito Lopesunder the guidance of the teacher [Ana Cecília Soja](https://integra.iff.edu.br/p/ana-cecilia-soja), we present the project **"[[en/research/satellite-trail-removal|Simulando o impacto de satélites em observações astronômicas]]"**— an AI algorithm that removes satellite tracks from astronomical data, recovering 99.7% of the information that would be lost. We were invited, accredited by [[febic-2024| FEBIC 2024]], when we won third place in the category of Higher Education with this same project. We stayed in**1st place in the Exact Sciences category of Higher Education**, which ranked us for the National Youth Science event in Recife (PE), in 2026.

To know a region so remote and distant from us, the extremely rich and interesting culture, all the affection of the organization of the fair to the participants, everything was very cool!
