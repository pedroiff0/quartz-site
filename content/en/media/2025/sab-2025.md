# 🔭 SAB 2025

> [!note] Summary
> Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Astronomical Society in Caxambu (MG) — I presented "Women in the Skies" (oral) and the poster on detection of anomalies in data from Gaia/GALAH DR4.

## 🗓️ About the event

 Page dedicated to participation in the Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Astronomical Society (SAB 2025)**, in Caxambu (MG), from September 28 to October 2, 2025 — the largest research meeting in astronomy in Latin America, with about 500 participants and more than 200 papers presented.

 Next to the teacher [Ana Cecília Soja](https://integra.iff.edu.br/p/ana-cecilia-soja), presented the work **"[[pt-br/research/anomaly-detection/index|Stellar properties and chemical features of the Gaia Catalogue of Nearby Stars observed by GALAH DR4]]"** (poster, analysis of about 6,000 stars with data from Gaia satellite).

> [!note] Opinion
> That was my first participation in national congress. It was extremely interesting to note that the purpose is very different from a Science Fair (such as those that took part in previous years). As I summed up at the time: "at a congress there is no competition, you just want to show that you are doing science... but collaboratively." It is very nice to present the work to people who understand the area and who can certainly contribute, even if minimally to the science and construction of it. I had the opportunity to meet researchers who accompany the work, I was invited to a group of "Journal Club", Milky Way (MilkWayBR), and I was very happy to be in an environment focused on Astronomy. Since I am a student of the countryside, topics like this are unspoken, and so it is very magical to be in a place that talk about!
