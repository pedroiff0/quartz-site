# SAB

> [!note] Summary
> Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Astronomical Society, in Caxambu (MG) — I presented "Women in the Skies" (oral) and the poster on anomaly detection in Gaia/GALAH DR4 data.

## About the event

Page dedicated to my participation in the **Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Astronomical Society (SAB 2025)**, in Caxambu (MG), from 28 September to 2 October 2025 — the largest astronomy research meeting in Latin America, with about 500 participants and more than 200 works presented.

Alongside Professor [Ana Cecília Soja](https://integra.iff.edu.br/p/ana-cecilia-soja), I presented the work **"[[en/research/deteccao-de-anomalias-iff-2025/index|Stellar properties and chemical features of the Gaia Catalogue of Nearby Stars observed by GALAH DR4]]"** (poster, analysis of about 6,000 stars with data from the Gaia satellite).

> [!note] Opinion
> This was my first participation in a national conference. It was extremely interesting to notice that the purpose is very different from a science fair (like the ones I took part in in previous years). As I summed it up at the time: "at a conference there is no competition, you just want to show that you are doing science... but collaboratively.". It is really nice to present the work to people who understand the field and who can certainly contribute, even if minimally, to the science and to the building of it. I had the chance to meet researchers whose work I follow, I was invited to a Journal Club on the Milky Way (MilkyWayBR), and I was very happy to be in an environment focused on Astronomy. As I am a student from the countryside, topics like this are rarely discussed, and that is why it is so magical to be in a place where they are!
