# 🎮 e-JINIFF 2024

> [!note] Summary
> I organized the Itabapoana Good Jesus campus e-sport team in the institutional stage of the Federal Network Electronic Games — 1st place in Valorant, 3rd in Free Fire.

## 🗓️ About the event

 Page dedicated to the institutional stage of the **e-JINIFF 2024 (Federal Network Electronic Games)**, organized by the Cabo Frio Campus with the participation of several IFF units in August 2024.

 I was the organizer of the Itabapoana Bom Jesus campus team, which competed in five sport modalities (in addition to a chess tournament). We won the 1st place in Valorant**, with Marcus Vinicius (Technical Course in Informatics), and the 3rd place in Free Fire**. We also participated in League of Legends, although no victory. The idea now is to expand the campus e-sports with training projects and a tournament of its own in Computer Week.

> [!note] Opinion
> Despite the chaos of games, it was interesting to see that the community is also interested in electronic games! Unfortunately for lack of support we did not bring participation forward, but it was a good experience!
