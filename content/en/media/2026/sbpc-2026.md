# 🧪 SBPC - 2026

> [!note] Summary
> Participation in the 78th Annual Meeting of the SBPC in Niterói - RJ. Presentation of the poster  "Stellar Properties and Chemical Characteristics of Gaia Catalog of Nearby Stars observed by GALAH DR4"  and follow-up of debates on scientific sovereignty and artificial intelligence in education.

## 🗓️ About the event

 Page dedicated to participation in the 78th Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Society for the Advancement of Science (SBPC 2026)**, held at the Fluminense Federal University (UFF) in Niterói – RJ, from July 26 to August 1, 2026.

## 👋 My participation

 I presented the work of stellar and chemical properties, integrating astrometric data from Gaia (GCNS) and high resolution spectroscopic data from GALAH DR4.

 In addition to presenting the poster, I actively participated in the activities and exhibitions at ExpoT\&C, visiting the booths of state-of-the-art institutions such as CBPF, ON, MCTI and LNCC, narrowing contacts and getting to know national scientific initiatives.

> [!note] Opinion
> The Annual Meeting of the SBPC was an experience of immense academic and personal value. Unlike congresses focused exclusively on astrophysics, the diversity of topics in the SBPC allowed me to expand my vision of the social role of science, technology and national data sovereignty.
>
> The UFF infrastructure proved to be excellent, with a very dynamic flow of accreditation, lectures and booths. In addition, the ease of mobility in Niterói (using the NitBike public bicycle system) allowed for a very nice integration between the event schedule and the knowledge of the region. Understanding the scientific policies under construction in the country renewed my motivation for basic research.
