# SAB 2026 Annual Meeting

> [!abstract] Summary
> XLIX Annual Meeting of the Brazilian Astronomical Society (RASAB 2026), in Natal - RN, from 25/10/2026 to 30/10/2026. I bring the poster that continues the SAB 2025 work: last year I looked only at the catalogue numbers; now I ask what the whole spectrum of each star shows.

> [!tip] Research behind the poster
> Results, poster figures and the history of the project: [[en/research/deteccao-de-anomalias-iff-2025/index|Spectral Anomaly Detection in Stars (GALAH DR4)]].

### Highlighted results

SAB 2025 described the Solar neighbourhood only through catalogue numbers. SAB 2026 asks what the whole spectrum adds, and these are the results, the same as in the "SAB 2026: the continuation" section of the [[en/research/deteccao-de-anomalias-iff-2025/index|research note]].

- **Sample:** 6,198 stars from GCNS x GALAH DR4. 5,304 enter the map; the other 894 lack the full spectrum from the four cameras and are never filled in.
- **Ordering by temperature:** it appears in t-SNE and UMAP without labels, in every configuration tested.
- **Emission stars:** there are 286 and they form five smaller groups. The largest (138 stars) is made of cool stars with metals similar to the Sun's; two others are metal-poor.
- **Cuts only filter:** the signal and temperature cuts change what is shown, they do not redo the map, and 52% of the mapped stars carry the low-signal flag.
- **Candidates, not classes:** the clustering settings and the cuts have not yet been fully tested.
- **Next step:** take the analysis to the whole of GALAH DR4 (905,415 spectra already extracted).
