## Summary

 Classical article of chemical evolution: uses literature models to test nucleosynthesis of elements. It shows that iron comes from SN Ia (long-life stars) and carbon from low-mass stars, explaining \[O/Fe] high halo stars. Theoretical basis of the abundances you use.

 [See original article](https://doi.org/10.1086/157039)
