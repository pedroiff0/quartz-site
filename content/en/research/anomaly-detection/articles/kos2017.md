## Summary

 It describes the GALAH survey data reduction pipeline (HERMES spectrograph, R=28,000, 29 element target, 1 million stars). The pipeline produces calibrated spectra to estimate atmospheric parameters. It is fundamental because the chemical abundances of GALAH feed its design for detecting anomalies.

 [See original article](https://doi.org/10.1093/mnras/stw2064)·[📄 Baixar PDF](/assets/articles/Kos2017.pdf)
