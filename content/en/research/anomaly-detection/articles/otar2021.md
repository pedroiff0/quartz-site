## Summary

 It has neural autoencoder that extracts latent spectra characteristics and reconstructs the spectrum; trained with non-peculiar spectra, it identifies emissions comparing observed vs generated. He discovered 10,364 candidates with Hα/Hβ emission at HERMES. Another example of self - encoding to detect spectral peculiarities — straight to its method of anomalies.
