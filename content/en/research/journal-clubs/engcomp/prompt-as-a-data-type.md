> [!note] In short
> A database that treats LLM prompts as a type of relational data, allowing the system to rewrite, optimize and manage them natively.

 Martins, D. M. L. & Vossen, G. (2026) 

## Summary
 Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly used with relational databases, but prompts are usually isolated outside the bank (in code), invisible for the optimization of query and metadata. The "PromptDB" introduces the type of logical data `PROMPT`, allowing the database optimizer to rewrite the prompts to improve the cost-benefit and accuracy of the use of LLMs.
