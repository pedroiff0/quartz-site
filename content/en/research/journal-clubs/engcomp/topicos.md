## 🔒 Security

- [cs.CR — Cryptography and Security](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CR/recent)— encryption, protocols, vulnerabilities, privacy, security of systems and networks. It is the closest category to what is seen in information security disciplines.

## 🧱 Software and Language Engineering

- [cs.SE — Software Engineering](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.SE/recent)— testing, maintenance, technical debt, repository mining, empirical studies with developers. Much article here is readable without heavy math — good starting point for those who have never read paper.
- [cs.PL — Programming Languages](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.PL/recent)— semantics, type systems, compilers, verification. More formal than cs.SE, and the natural complement of Compiler discipline.

## 🧮 Algorithms and Theory

- [cs.DS — Data Structures and Algorithms](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.DS/recent)— design and analysis of algorithms, data structures, applied complexity. Direct home with Data Structure and Algorithms Design and Analysis.

## 🤖 Artificial Intelligence

- [cs.AI — Artificial Intelligence](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AI/recent)— umbrella category: agents, planning, reasoning, applications.
- [cs.LG — Machine Learning](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.LG/recent)— the bulk of machine learning volume. High daily volume; filter by title before opening.
- [cs.CL — Computing and Language](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.CL/recent)— natural language processing and language models.

## 🖧 Systems and Networks

- [cs.DC — Distributed, Parallel, and Cluster Computing](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.DC/recent)— distributed and parallel computing, consensus, fault tolerance, HPC.
- [cs.OS — Operating Systems](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.OS/recent)— scheduling, memory, kernels, virtualization. Small category, you can sweep the entire list.
- [cs.NI — Networking and Internet Architecture](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.NI/recent)— protocols, network architecture, traffic measurement.

## 🔌 Hardware and Signals

- [cs.AR — Hardware Architecture](https://arxiv.org/list/cs.AR/recent)— processor architecture, accelerators, hardware/software co-project.
- [eess.SP — Signal Processing](https://arxiv.org/list/eess.SP/recent)— signal processing, the electrical engineering side of the course.

> [!tip] Do you want to follow without visiting the site?
> ArXiv publishes a feed by category: `https://rss.arxiv.org/rss/cs.SE` (change category). You can sign on an RSS reader and receive the new titles every day. The feed is empty on weekends and holidays, when the arXiv does not announce submissions.

## How to choose an article to present

 Three criteria that avoid most problems:

1. *Head in a week. If you can't read and understand enough to explain in 20 minutes, it's an article for another time. 60-page preprint with 30 appendix rarely works.
2. *The method is reproducible or at least verifiable. ** Article describing what you did in such a way that you can question yields discussion; article that only announces result, no.
3. **Interests more than one person.** The point is the conversation after the presentation. A hyperspecific article from your monograph is great for you and warm for the group.

 It doesn't have to be an article of the week. Classic of the area, an article you did not understand and want help to untangle, or paper that supports your TCC — everything goes, as long as it fits the three criteria.
